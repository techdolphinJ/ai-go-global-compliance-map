# Breadth-Tier Compliance Map

**Markets:** United Kingdom, India, Viet Nam, Thailand, Malaysia, Mexico, Saudi Arabia, Nigeria, Australia, Canada

**Source standard:** public first-party legal, regulatory, government, or official-legislative material only
**Verification date for every entry:** **2026-07-31**

## Purpose and reading rules

This is an intentionally lighter **breadth tier**, not an incomplete depth-tier map. Each market is anchored only to the two launch-critical dimensions most likely to constrain an AI product: **data compliance** and **AI regulatory status**. The three other dimensions are deliberately marked **Not covered in breadth tier — see depth-tier methodology** rather than inferred from generic country knowledge.

- **✅ Official primary source**: a public government, regulator, official legal database, or official legislative record is linked, with a source/version year and verification date.
- **🟡 Official policy or legislative proposal only**: the linked record is official, but it is not an enacted, comprehensive AI-specific law. It must not be treated as one.
- A government strategy, guideline, or bill is never relabelled as a licence, registration rule, or effective AI Act.
- “No enacted AI-specific law asserted” is a scoped source-status finding. It does not remove sectoral, consumer, cybersecurity, content, or provincial/state obligations.

## Source-status exceptions

- **No entry relies on a secondary source.** Mexico and Canada are marked **🟡** because the only AI-specific materials used for the status statement are official legislative records of proposals, not effective AI laws.
- Thailand’s data-law link is hosted by a government ministry but labels its English text an **unofficial translation**. The entry remains linked to the underlying official statute and instructs readers to use the Thai text for controlling interpretation.
- The three non-anchored dimensions in every breadth-tier market are an intentional coverage boundary, not an assessment that no rule applies.

## Scope and disclaimer

This map is a traceable research aid, **not legal advice**. It is not a substitute for checking the latest official text, implementation notices, subnational rules, sector-specific requirements, and a product’s actual data, payment, sales, and operating model before launch.
