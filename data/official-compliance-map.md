# Official Compliance Map

**Markets:** European Union, South Korea, Singapore, Indonesia, Japan, United States, Brazil, United Arab Emirates

**Source standard:** public first-party legal or regulatory material only
**Verification date for every entry:** **2026-07-31**

## Reading this map

- **Effective law** means the cited source is a binding legal or regulatory text. It does not mean every obligation is already applicable to every AI product.
- **Government guideline** means an official source, but not a generic mandatory AI-service licence.
- **Official legal baseline** means a sector-neutral rule (for example, electronic-system registration or payment regulation) that can matter to an AI product.
- The map does not infer a “no requirement” answer from the absence of a cited rule. Each launch needs product-, customer-, and business-model-specific review.
