# Market Operations — Non-Official Sources

> **Non-official source layer — reference only. Do not treat this file as legal authority.**

## Why this is separate

Mainstream payment methods, acquiring approval rates, settlement practices, and payment-processor availability are market-operating observations. They are not regulations, and they should never be mixed into the official legal-source map.

## V1 status

This first release intentionally contains **no market-operation claims**. No secondary source is needed to explain an official legal requirement, and no payment-method preference is presented as a compliance rule.

When this layer is populated, every entry must include:

| Required field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Source class | Must say `non-official` in the entry itself. |
| Publisher and URL | Name the publisher and link directly to the dated material. |
| Geography and audience | State the exact market and whether data is consumer, merchant, B2B, or B2C. |
| Data period | State the measurement period; a publication date alone is insufficient. |
| Use limitation | Say that the source is contextual market information, not legal advice or regulatory authority. |

The legal entry point for payment and funds remains in [official-compliance-map.md](official-compliance-map.md).
